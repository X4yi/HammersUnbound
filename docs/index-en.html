Combat Mod for Minecraft 1.12.2

Hammers Unbound

Two hammer types. Unique abilities. Melee combat reimagined with stun, area damage, bleeding and blood pacts.

Forge 1.12.2 Version r1.0 Client + Server

What is it?

Hammers Unbound adds two hammer lines with completely different combat mechanics. Each material (wood, stone, iron, gold, diamond) scales in power and abilities.

Warhammers control the battlefield with stun and area damage. Spikehammers wear down victims with stacking bleeding and drain their life through a blood pact.
