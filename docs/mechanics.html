Warhammer — Crowd Control

Warhammer

Critical Hit: Stun + AoE

When landing a critical hit with a Warhammer, two effects trigger simultaneously:

  • Stun on primary target: The enemy is completely immobilized for a duration determined by the hammer's material. They cannot move, attack, or use abilities.
  • Area damage (AoE): A shockwave expands from the impact point, damaging and briefly stunning all nearby enemies within the hammer's radius.

The shockwave generates an explosion sound and a visual crack effect on the ground, along with signature impact particles.

📷 Screenshot: Warhammer hitting a group of mobs, showing the shockwave and stunned enemies Recommended resolution: 1920x1080, F1 to hide HUD

Tip: The Diamond Warhammer has the largest AoE radius and longest stun duration. Ideal for controlling entire hordes.

Spikehammer — Wear Down & Drain

Spikehammer

Stacking Bleeding

The Spikehammer applies bleeding to enemies with every critical hit or sprint hit (as long as the attack is fully charged). Bleeding works as follows:

  • Stacking levels: Each hit adds a bleeding level, up to a maximum determined by the material.
  • Damage per tick: Bleeding deals periodic magic damage. Higher levels mean more damage and faster ticks.
  • Decay: If not refreshed, bleeding loses levels gradually until it disappears.
  • Blood particles: The effect generates dark blood particles that spray from the target, providing clear visual feedback.
📷 Screenshot: Enemy with multiple bleeding levels active, showing blood particles Recommended resolution: 1920x1080, F1 to hide HUD
Spikehammer

Blood Pact

By right-clicking with a Spikehammer in hand, you activate the Blood Pact:

  • Distance tether: A visual link is created between you and the target enemy.
  • Life drain: The pact periodically drains life from the enemy and heals you with a percentage of the drained damage.
  • Limited range: If you move too far from the target, the tether breaks.
  • Bonus healing on hit: While the pact is active, every hit you land on the enemy heals you additionally.

The pact deactivates automatically if the enemy dies, if you move too far away, or if you die.

📷 Screenshot: Active Blood Pact showing the visual tether between player and enemy Recommended resolution: 1920x1080, F1 to hide HUD

Note: Gold has the highest drain percentage but the lowest durability. Diamond has the longest range and fastest drain.

Visual Effects

Visual

AoE Impact Particles

When the Warhammer triggers its area effect, a visible shockwave is generated on the ground with particles radiating from the impact point. The effect includes an explosion sound and a block breaking sound.

Visual

Blood Particles

Bleeding generates dark red particles that spray from the target and fall to the ground with realistic physics. The visual intensity scales with the active bleeding level.
